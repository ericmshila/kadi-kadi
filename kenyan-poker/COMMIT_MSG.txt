feat: delay and soften the legal-card hint so a hand still requires thinking

The legal-card preview used to dim every unplayable card the instant
it became your turn — which handed over the whole hand's answer at a
glance and made a solo game feel like watching two AIs play each
other. Two changes:

1. Dimming is gone entirely. A card that isn't currently playable now
   renders exactly like any other card — no opacity/saturation
   penalty. PlayingCard's `legal` prop is effectively positive-only
   now (`true` glows, anything else is plain); the old
   `.illegal-play` styling and CSS are removed.

2. The remaining hint — a subtle, gently pulsing gold glow on
   playable cards — is withheld for the first 3 seconds of every new
   decision (Table.tsx's new `hintsRevealed` timer), giving the
   player a moment to actually look at their hand and think before
   any assist appears. The timer restarts on a fresh turn, a
   mid-turn phase change (e.g. you play a question card and
   immediately owe an answer), or your hand size changing (you just
   drew) — and is correctly gated behind the shuffle-overlay
   animation too, so the countdown doesn't secretly burn down while
   the hand is still hidden behind it.

Verified: tsc/build/lint clean, and a live Playwright pass confirming
no glow and no dimming at 0s or 1.5s into a turn, a glow appearing by
~3.5s, and zero illegal-play elements ever rendered at any point.
